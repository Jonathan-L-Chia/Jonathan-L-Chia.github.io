Welcome to ENDLS (**E**xamining **N**ovel **D**ynamics in **L**ongitudinal Well-being and **S**ubjective Experiences)!

Our lab investigates how well-being and other subjective experiences unfold and interrelate across the life course. Using advanced quantitative methods, we examine the dynamic processes that shape psychological well-being over time — with particular attention to aging, historical and cohort differences, life events, and the methodological challenges of capturing change accurately.

ENDLS collaborates with international researchers and draws on major panel studies to pursue these questions at scale. We welcome students and collaborators interested in longitudinal methodology and the study of well-being across the life course.

About Myself
======
My name is Dr. Jonathan L. Chia and I lead ENDLS. I am a former presidential doctoral scholar and scholar at the Centre for Research on Successful Ageing (ROSA). Currently, I am a Lecturer in Psychology at James Cook University, and an incoming Section Editor at *Gerontology*. After completing my PhD at Singapore Management University, I undertook my post-doctoral fellowship at Humboldt University of Berlin, under the mentorship of leading developmental psychologist Dr. Denis Gerstorf. My research interests lie at the intersection of advanced longitudinal methodologies and well-being in later life, with a focus on purpose in life.
